What the agenda words mean

Public hearing. The committee takes public testimony on the bill. This is your chance to speak, send written comments or sign in for or against it.

Executive session. The committee votes on whether to move the bill forward. There's no public testimony, but you can watch.

Work session. A briefing for the committee, usually from agencies or experts. No votes and usually no public testimony.

Ready to weigh in?

Use the Legislature’s Committee Sign-In page to sign in with your position, send written testimony or sign up to testify in person or remotely. Sign-in closes before the meeting starts, so don’t wait until the last minute.
